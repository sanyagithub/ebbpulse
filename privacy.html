Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Ebb Pulse is a breathing app for Apple Watch. It is built to keep your information on your Watch.

What the app does with your data

Heart rate

If you grant access, Ebb Pulse reads your heart rate from Apple Health during a breathing session and shows it on your Watch, along with the lowest and highest readings of that session. The readings are kept in memory only while the session runs and are not stored, shared or sent anywhere.

Workout session

While you breathe, the app runs a short on-wrist session so your Watch keeps the app in front and can measure heart rate. It is always discarded when the session ends. Nothing is saved to the Health app.

On-device settings and progress

Your breathing pattern, session length, daily goal, reminder times, and your count of completed sessions and streak are stored only on your Watch.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules notifications on your Watch. They are created and delivered on the device; no server is involved.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect, transmit or sell personal information.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Your choices

You can withdraw Health access at any time in the Health settings on your iPhone or Watch (Privacy → Apps → Ebb Pulse), and turn off reminders in the app's Settings. Deleting the app removes its on-device data.

Not medical advice

Ebb Pulse is for relaxation and general wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you feel dizzy or lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
